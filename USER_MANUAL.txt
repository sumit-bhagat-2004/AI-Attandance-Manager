3. CORE FEATURES
================================================================================

ATTENDANCE TRACKING
-------------------
The heart of EduTrack AI is its intelligent attendance tracking system.

Marking Attendance:
1. Daily Attendance:
   - View your daily class schedule on the main dashboard
   - Each class appears as a card with subject details
   - Click on any class to mark attendance status:
     * GREEN = Present
     * RED = Absent  
     * GRAY = Not marked yet

2. Bulk Operations:
   - "Mark All Present" button for days with full attendance
   - "Mark All Absent" button for sick days or holidays
   - Individual correction by clicking specific classes

3. Attendance History:
   - Complete history available in Calendar view
   - Visual indicators for each day's performance
   - Monthly and weekly attendance patterns
   - Exportable attendance reports

Color Coding System:
- Green: Present (good attendance)
- Red: Absent (requires attention)
- Gray: Unmarked (needs input)
- Yellow: Warning (approaching attendance threshold)

Weekly Schedule:

Schedule Customization:

MAKEUP CLASSES

7. TROUBLESHOOTING
================================================================================

COMMON ISSUES AND SOLUTIONS
---------------------------

Issue: Login Problems
Solution:
- Check internet connection
- Clear browser cache and cookies
- Try incognito/private browsing mode
- Contact support if problem persists

Issue: Attendance Not Saving
Solution:
- Refresh the page and try again
- Check internet connectivity
- Ensure you're clicking the save button
- Try logging out and logging back in

Issue: Reports Not Generating
Solution:
- Check that you have attendance data
- Ensure stable internet connection
- Wait a few minutes and try again
- Clear browser cache

Issue: Mobile App Not Loading
Solution:
- Check mobile data/WiFi connection
- Update your mobile browser
- Clear browser data
- Try accessing from desktop

Issue: Schedule Not Displaying
Solution:
- Refresh the page
- Check if you're in the correct semester
- Verify your academic schedule setup
- Contact admin if schedule is incorrect

PERFORMANCE OPTIMIZATION
------------------------
For best performance:
1. Use modern browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
2. Keep browser updated
3. Clear cache regularly
4. Close unnecessary browser tabs
5. Ensure stable internet connection

DATA BACKUP
-----------
Your data is automatically backed up, but you can also:
1. Export attendance reports regularly
2. Save important reports locally
3. Document important achievements
4. Keep academic records updated

8. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
================================================================================

Q: How accurate are the AI recommendations?
A: The AI analyzes your attendance patterns, subject performance, and academic 
trends to provide highly personalized recommendations. While the system is very 
accurate, always consult with your academic advisors for important decisions.

Q: Can I edit attendance after marking it?
A: Yes, you can change attendance status by clicking on the class again and 
selecting the correct status. The system maintains a history of changes.

Q: How do makeup classes work?
A: When you miss classes, the system automatically identifies makeup 
requirements and suggests available opportunities. You can schedule and track 
makeup classes through the Makeup section.

Q: Can I export my data?
A: Yes, you can export attendance reports and statistics through the Reports 
section. This is useful for sharing with advisors or keeping personal records.

Q: Is my data secure?
A: Yes, all data is encrypted and stored securely using industry-standard 
security practices. We follow strict privacy policies to protect your information.

Q: Can I use this on multiple devices?
A: Yes, EduTrack AI is a web application that works on any device with an 
internet connection and modern web browser.

Q: What if I make a mistake in marking attendance?
A: You can easily correct mistakes by clicking on the class again and selecting 
the correct status. Changes are saved automatically.

Q: How often should I generate AI reports?
A: We recommend generating reports weekly for best results. This provides timely 
insights while giving the AI enough data to make meaningful recommendations.

Q: Can I share my progress with parents or advisors?
A: Yes, you can export reports and statistics to share with parents, advisors, 
or anyone else who needs to track your academic progress.

Q: What happens if I miss several days of classes?
A: The system will automatically identify all missed classes and provide 
comprehensive makeup recommendations. The AI will also generate recovery plans 
to help you catch up.
